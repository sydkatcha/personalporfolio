Hi! My name is Sydney. I am a graphic designer and artist with a passion for physical experimentation. I am a firm believer that awesome things happen when you try that crazy idea, whether it's using an out-of-the-box tool or approaching a media piece from a unique angle. I have experience with drawing, painting, UX/UI, and digital tools like Adobe Photoshop, InDesign, and Illustrator, DaVinci Resolve, Figma, and Procreate/Adobe Fresco. As a designer, I am an analytical thinker, and I love identifying patterns in order to organize information logically. Design tends to feel like a big creative logic problem! My goal is to make work that is intuitive and helpful for its audience.

I am particularly interested in molecular biology (structure=function!!) and language (I'm fluent in Spanish and I've studied abroad in Mexico!), and on any given day you’ll usually find me climbing, reading, playing violin (I’m a-ight), rollerblading, blasting broadway musical recordings on repeat, or wrapped in some sort of creative endeavor (perhaps creating long chains of origami…). I also love cinematography and studying film, and I am currently experimenting with color-grading and editing in DaVinci Resolve!
