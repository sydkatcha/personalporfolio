about


Hi! My name is Sydney. I am a graphic designer and artist with a passion for physical experimentation. I am a firm believer that awesome things happen when you try that crazy idea, whether it's beating a piece of paper with a charcoal-covered sandal to create a dynamic drawing or ripping up magazine scraps to create a quirky album cover. I have experience with drawing, painting, UX/UI, and digital tools. As a designer, I am an analytical thinker, and I love identifying patterns and getting to the bottom of a message. My goal in graphic design is to make things that are intuitive and helpful for their viewers or users.

I am particularly interested in biology (form=function!!), and on any given day you’ll usually find me climbing, reading, playing violin (I’m a-ight), rollerblading, blasting broadway musical recordings on repeat, or wrapped in some sort of creative endeavor (perhaps creating long chains of origami…). Abstract shapes and I have a pretty solid relationship, and I’m slightly obsessed with painting them on wood scraps I find. I currently hold the self-proclaimed title of wood-scrap garbage disposal of the universe. No biggie.
