Menu Redesign : Little Bread Company | 2026


This project was all about redesigning a local Fayetteville, AR cafe menu with an emphasis on typography. The goal was to create a menu with a unique, ecclectic feel that could be read on a single page with appropriate hierarchy. The menu was designed in Adobe Illustrator and is available in three different color varieties.

Boulders, Brews + Bananas | 2026

Project focused on creating flyers/posters and a t-shirt design for the 2026 Halloween Community Bouldering Competition at Boulders & Brews Climbing and Coffee in Fayetteville, AR. Being minion-themed, the competition advertising needed to hint to minions without using copyrighted content. The typography was hand-printed on paper and manipulated in Illustrator.

Format Design : Acts and Allegories | 2026

Acts and Allegories is a multi-month class project I had the privelege of leading as project manager alongside design manager Cara Spratlin. The 8x10in 266 pg book recounts Aesop's fables through the lens of historical circus photography of the 1850s-1950s. It divides Aesop's fables into 5 distinct chapters: the ringmaster, the beast, the performer, the worker, and the trickster, each representing Aesop's respective moral message groups: power and authority, true nature and instinct, pride and hubris, hard work and resilience, and deception and wit. The book's design heavily focuses on specific colors, textures, typefaces, and metaphors that compliment each chapter's message.

App Layout Design : MySpot Parking App, 2026

MySpot is a cross-platform Figma app prototype created by a group of graphic design students for University of Arkansas students to help ease parking anxiety. The app theoretically collects data around campus and allows its user to view availability of free spots and report observations. Its design features easily readable typefaces paired with simple icons and a very limited color pallete.
