Small Book Design : "This Is Water" by David Foster Wallace, 2026

In this project, I sought to distill the graduation speech "This Is Water" by David Foster Wallace into a small book format using a single typeface, single interior color, and three typeface weights/widths. Wallace's speech features simple language, sarcasm, and an array of analogies. The cover design contains purposefully rotated text to visually demonstrate his message of consciously noticing one's own automatic assumptions and looking at seemingly mundane daily events from a different perspective. He challenges the audience to notice their own biases and remember that, like fish to the existence of water, we may be oblivious to our own inner assumptions.

Book Cover : Flowers for Algernon, 2025

Flowers for Algernon by Daniel Keyes follows a character named Charlie who undergoes experimentation to increase his IQ. The book explores how "intelligence without the ability to give and receive affection leads to mental and moral breakdown." In designing the cover, I was inspired by the Rorschach inkblot cards, by which Charlie's neurological changes are occasionally measured in the story. The cover features a muted, cool-toned color scheme, and the illustrations nod to Charlie's mouse companion, Algernon, who is also subjected to the same tests alongside him.



Album Cover : Next to Normal, 2026

This album cover design features the 2009 Original Braodway Cast Recording album Next to Normal, which follows a suburban family struggling with the loss of a son and the challenges of mental healthcare and psychopharmacology. The musical's songs are gritty, leaning towards the rock genre. The final design combines a blue, melancholic mood with rough texture, a chromatic purple secondary color, the “side effects may vary” phrase, as well as the counter-clockwise rotation of the song titles. My goal was to create a visually balanced design that hinted to pharmaceuticals, instability, and grief, while still having a “broadway musical aesthetic.”
