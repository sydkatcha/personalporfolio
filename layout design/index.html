Menu Redesign : Little Bread Company

This project was all about redesigning a local Fayetteville, AR cafe with an emphasis on typography. The goal was to create a menu with a unique, ecclectic feel that could be read on a single page with appropriate hierarchy. The menu is available in three different color varieties.


Small Book Design : This Is Water by David Foster Wallace

This project sought to represent the graduation speech "This Is Water" by David Foster Wallace in a small book format using a single typeface, single interior color, and three typeface weights/widths. Wallace's speech features simple language, sarcasm, and an array of analogies. The booklet design contains purposefully rotated text to visually demonstrate his message of consciously noticing one's own automatic assumptions and looking at seemingly mundane daily events from a different perspective. He challenges the audience to notice their own biases and remember that, like fish to the existence of water, we may be oblivious to our own inner assumptions.


Format Design : Acts and Allegories

"Acts and Allegories" is a multi-month class project I had the privelege of leading as project manager alongside design manager Cara Spratlin. The 8x10in 266 pg book recounts Aesop's fables through the lens of historical 1850-1950 circus photography. It divides Aesop's fables into 5 distinct chapters: the ringmaster, the beast, the performer, the worker, and the trickster, each representing Aesop's respective moral message groups: power and authority, true nature and instinct, pride and hubris, hard work and resilience, and deception and wit. The book's design heavily focuses on specific colors, textures, typefaces, and metaphors that compliment each chapter's message.
